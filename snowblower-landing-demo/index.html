Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

Nope! As long as the snowblower is left outside in an accessible location (like your driveway or beside the garage), we can load it up and leave a confirmation text.

How long does a shop repair take?

Most shop repairs are completed within 3 to 5 business days, depending on parts availability. We will always communicate the timeline when we provide your written estimate.

What if my machine needs more than a tune-up?

If our technician arrives for an At-Home Tune-Up and discovers a major issue, we will simply apply the tune-up fee toward a full Shop Repair and haul it back with us.
